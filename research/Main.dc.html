Planning & demolitions7 Oct 2026Rahat desk

Bulldozers reach Zyadna, and Rahat’s Nova rescuer loses his sheds and his nephew’s home

Dozens of bulldozers came to the Bedouin neighbourhood beside Rahat this week. Among the structures taken down: Yousef Zyadna’s warehouse and chicken coop, and the house of a nephew who had recently married.

Zyadna, a minibus driver, went to the Nova festival site on the morning of 7 October 2023 and drove people out. Israel later honoured him for it. Four of his relatives were taken into Gaza that day; Bilal and Aisha came home in November 2023, and the bodies of Hamza and Yousef were returned in January 2025.

None of that kept the demolition crews away. Local Call, which first reported the operation, counts 65 demolition orders against homes in the neighbourhood.

Opposition MK Naama Lazimi of the Democrats called the demolition “a moral and ethical disgrace.” National Security Minister Itamar Ben Gvir has made Negev demolitions a pillar of his ministry, describing them as restoring “governance.”

The reports do not say which authority signed the orders. Zyadna told Local Call he does not regret what he did on 7 October: “these are our values.”

Segev Shalom

شقيب السلام Northern Naqab · local council Filed 8 Oct

Shin Bet says five townsmen were arrested in the spring over an alleged plot

The security service said on Thursday that it detained five residents of Segev Shalom in May and June, alleging they planned to form a “military battalion” and carry out attacks. The announcement came two days after the agency’s chief briefed ministers on the Naqab.

Haaretz, 8 Oct 2026

Hura

حورة Northern Naqab · local council Filed 7 Oct

“There is no such thing among us”: Hura’s council head answers the Shin Bet

Shin Bet chief David Zini told ministers this week of “very worrying” signs of militant organising in unrecognised Bedouin villages, and the prime minister asked for a plan. Habes al-Atawna, head of the Hura Local Council, rejected it to Walla and called the timing, weeks before the election, suspicious. “There is criminality among a small minority,” he said, but not terrorism. The Regional Council of Unrecognised Villages called the warning “a total spin.”

Jerusalem Post, 7 Oct 2026, citing Walla · i24NEWS, 6 Oct 2026

“It cannot be viewed merely as an issue of policing and enforcement.”

Ra’ed al-Qian, activist, Hura, to the Jerusalem Post
03

Haifa

حيفا The coast · Wadi Nisnas, Halisa, Abbas St Last filed 1 Oct

From a Haifa legal centre, a count of students put on trial by their colleges

Adalah, the rights centre based in the city, says 132 Palestinian students who faced disciplinary proceedings after 7 October 2023 asked it for help, 101 of them women. Its cases span 40 of Israel’s 61 accredited institutions. Close to home: the University of Haifa’s rector suspended six Arab students on 8 October 2023, suspensions its own committees reversed in January 2024.

04

Kafr Qasim

كفر قاسم Southern Triangle · population 27,996 (2024) Date ahead: 29 Oct

Seventy years on, 29 October falls two days after a national vote

The town marks seventy years since the Kafr Qasim massacre of 29 October 1956, when Border Police shot dead villagers returning from their fields under a curfew they had not been told about. This year the anniversary lands two days after the Knesset election.

Background: Wikipedia, “Kafr Qasim massacre”; election date, Wikipedia, “2026 Israeli legislative election”

Majd al-Krum

مجد الكروم Western Galilee · Shaghur Last filed 17 Sep

The settler camp is gone from the forest plot. The JNF says a smaller one comes back

Youths from the West Bank settlement of Tekoa pitched tents on a Jewish National Fund grazing plot inside the town’s boundaries in mid-September, wired to water and power the council says it never supplied. Hundreds of residents protested every evening; police arrested several. The JNF ordered the camp down, and chairman Eyal Ostrovsky admitted “mistakes and failures.” He also said grazing will resume after Yom Kippur from a relocated camp with no more than three people. Residents want their groves clear before the olive harvest.

Times of Israel 13 Sep · Middle East Eye 15 Sep · +972 Magazine 17 Sep
