A third of this year’s crime-fighting money for Arab towns was still unspent in mid-September

A Prime Minister’s Office document shows the National Security Ministry had used about two-thirds of its NIS 300 million for 2026. Roughly NIS 100 million remained. 8 OctCourts

Brother, cousin and father sentenced over the 2023 killing of Aya Abu Hajjaj in Lod

The Central District Court gave her brother, 14½ at the time, 22 years and a cousin 17 years for aggravated murder; her father got 15 years for aiding the murder. The three must pay more than NIS 300,000 to her two children. Prosecutors said she was killed for refusing to move back to her father’s family in Hura.

6 OctRoads

The other count: 134 Arab citizens killed on the roads in nine months

Road-safety group Or Yarok counts 134 Arab road deaths to 5 October, about 19% more than in the same period of 2025 — 39% of the 342 people killed on Israel’s roads, from a community that is about a fifth of the population. Fourteen died in September alone.

Source: Or Yarok data, Arab48, 6 Oct 2026.

Supreme Court unanimously lifts the election committee’s ban on Arab lists

Ra’am and the Joint List can stand on 27 October; Hadash MK Ofer Cassif was cleared by 7–2. Balad chairman Sami Abu Shehadeh withdrew after the court signalled it would uphold his own disqualification. Our election desk has the full story.

Source: Times of Israel, 2 Oct; JTA, 5 Oct 2026.

From the archive · Protest

The street kept its own count

It began in Sakhnin in late January, when shopkeepers shut their doors against protection rackets. Within days the High Follow-Up Committee had taken the strike nationwide, and on 31 January tens of thousands marched from Hostages Square to Habima Square — about half of them Arab, many on chartered buses.

Method

Whose count, and why one

We keep the Abraham Initiatives’ tally — citizens and permanent residents killed in crime and violence, including by police. Other monitors count differently. When they disagree we name the source; we never average.

Counts change after deaths in hospital; we correct the ledger, never overwrite it.
